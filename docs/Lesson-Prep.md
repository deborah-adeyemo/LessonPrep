**Lesson Prep is a one-person, teacher-owned app**. A teacher can prepare, edit, save, print, reuse, and improve lesson notes entirely independently.

# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Lesson Prep — Independent Nigerian Teacher Lesson Preparation App
**Document status:** Product definition  
**Product name:** Lesson Prep  
**Primary market:** Nigeria  
**Primary users:** Primary and Secondary school teachers  
**Product model:** Individual teacher-first application

# 1. Product Overview
**Lesson Prep** is an independent lesson-preparation app designed specifically for Nigerian teachers.

The app helps teachers create, organize, improve, reuse, and prepare lesson notes without requiring approval, supervision, or interference from a school administrator, HOD, department head, or other authority.

The teacher owns their preparation process.

The core experience is:

> **Choose what you are teaching → Create your lesson → Review it → Teach → Record what happened → Improve it → Prepare the next lesson.**

Lesson Prep combines:

* Nigerian lesson-note formats  
* Nigerian curriculum awareness  
* Schemes of work  
* Teacher-created content  
* Textbooks and references  
* AI-assisted lesson generation  
* Lesson history  
* Lesson continuity  
* Teaching activities  
* Teaching aids  
* Evaluation and assignments  
* Revision and remedial preparation  
* Assessment creation  
* Question banks  
* Reusable lesson components  
* Timetable and planning  
* Teacher reflections  
* Personal teaching resources

Everything is controlled by the individual teacher.

# 2. Product Vision
The vision for Lesson Prep is to give every teacher a personal digital lesson-preparation assistant that understands their teaching context without taking control of their professional decisions.

Lesson Prep should become the teacher's personal workspace for:

> **Planning → Preparing → Teaching → Reflecting → Improving → Reusing**

The product should feel like a teacher's own lesson-preparation notebook, teaching assistant, resource library, and planning companion in one place.

# 3. Core Product Philosophy
## Teacher First
Lesson Prep belongs to the teacher.

The teacher should never need to:

* Submit a lesson for approval  
* Wait for an HOD  
* Ask an administrator for permission  
* Share private lesson notes  
* Follow another person's workflow inside the app  
* Allow another person to modify their lesson  
* Use a school account to access their own preparation

The teacher decides what to create and how to use it.

# 4. Teacher Control
The teacher has complete control over their lesson notes.

They can:

* Create

* Restore  
* Print

* Organize  
* Annotate  
* Share externally if they choose

AI suggestions are optional.

Nothing generated by Lesson Prep should automatically become part of a teacher's final lesson without the teacher's acceptance.

* Administrative review  
* School-wide permissions  
* Official lesson locking

10. Teacher Activities  
11. Student Activities  
12. Teaching Methods  
13. Teaching Aids

15. Lesson Evaluation  
16. Conclusion

The teacher can:

* Add sections  
* Remove sections  
* Rename sections

# 9. Creating a Lesson
A teacher can begin with as little as:

> “JSS2 Mathematics — Algebra.”

If essential information is missing, the app asks only for what is necessary.

The teacher can also start with:

* A topic  
* A scheme of work  
* A textbook  
* A textbook page  
* A photograph  
* An old lesson note  
* A previous lesson  
* A blank template  
* A teaching idea

# 10. Preparation Modes
The teacher chooses how much assistance they want.

## Quick Prep
For teachers who need a lesson quickly.

Provides the essential lesson structure and content.

## Standard Prep
Creates a complete lesson note using the teacher's preferred format.

## Deep Prep
Adds:

* Detailed teaching content  
* Examples  
* Worked examples  
* Misconceptions  
* Alternative explanations  
* Differentiated activities  
* Teaching aids  
* Assessment ideas  
* Classroom support

The teacher can switch between modes at any time.

# 11. Create Without AI
Lesson Prep must also work as a normal lesson-note creation app.

A teacher should be able to create a lesson completely manually.

They can simply open a blank lesson note and type into the sections themselves.

AI is an assistant, not a requirement.

This is important because some teachers will want:

> **“I know exactly what I want to write. Just give me the structure.”**

Lesson Prep should support that workflow fully.

It should explain:

> “These sources differ on this point.”

The teacher then decides what to use.

Lesson Prep should never silently replace the teacher's selected material.

# 77. MVP Success Metrics
The MVP should demonstrate that Lesson Prep genuinely helps individual teachers prepare lessons.

### Primary targets
| Metric | Initial Target |
| ----- | ----- |
| Teachers completing their first lesson | ≥ 60% of activated teachers |
| Weekly active teachers | ≥ 50% of activated teachers |
| Lesson preparation completion | ≥ 70% of started lesson sessions |
| Median time to a usable lesson | ≤ 10 minutes |
| Lessons reusing previous material | ≥ 30% |
| Generated content accepted with minor/no changes | ≥ 70% |
| Teachers reporting meaningful time savings | ≥ 70% |
| Teachers reporting lesson output is usable | ≥ 80% |
| Four-week teacher retention | ≥ 40% |
| Teachers satisfied with teacher control | ≥ 80% |

### Nigerian lesson-format target
At least **80% of active teachers** should report that Lesson Prep's lesson-note structure fits their normal Nigerian lesson-preparation process.

### Continuity target
At least **50% of teachers who record post-lesson reflections** should subsequently use those reflections or previous lesson information when preparing another lesson.

# 78. MVP Success Definition
The MVP should be considered successful when teachers demonstrate that Lesson Prep has become part of their normal preparation routine.

Success means:

1. Teachers can create lessons manually without AI.  
2. Teachers can use AI when they want assistance.  
3. Teachers can create a usable Nigerian-format lesson quickly.  
4. Teachers return to prepare subsequent lessons.  
5. Teachers reuse previous lessons and components.  
6. Teachers use saved schemes and class context.  
7. Teachers report reduced preparation effort.  
8. Teachers retain complete control over their final lesson.  
9. Teachers can work privately without authority involvement.  
10. Teachers can connect previous lessons to future lessons.  
11. Teachers can reflect after teaching and use those reflections later.  
12. Teachers trust the app enough to make it part of their regular preparation workflow.

# 79. Product North Star
The central product promise is:

> **“Lesson Prep is your personal lesson-preparation assistant. It knows what you teach, who you teach, what you have already taught, how you like to prepare, and what happened in your previous lessons—and it helps you prepare the next lesson while leaving every decision in your hands.”**

The teacher should feel:

> **“This is my lesson notebook, my teaching assistant, and my resource library. I decide what goes into my lesson.”**

# 80. Final Product Positioning
**Lesson Prep is not a school management system.**

**It is not an HOD approval system.**

**It is not a teacher performance-monitoring system.**

**It is not primarily a student analytics system.**

It is a:

> **Personal Nigerian lesson-preparation and teaching-support app for teachers.**

Its core job is simple:

> **Help a teacher prepare a better lesson, faster, in their own way, and keep improving from one lesson to the next.**
