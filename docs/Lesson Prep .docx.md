

**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Lesson Prep — Nigerian Teacher Lesson Preparation & Teaching Support App**

**Document status:** Product definition **Product name:** Lesson Prep

**Primary market:** Nigeria

**Primary users:** Primary and Secondary school teachers  
**Secondary users:** HODs, department leaders, school administrators, teacher collaborators, resource contributors

**1\. Product Overview**

**Lesson Prep** is a teacher-focused lesson preparation and teaching support platform designed around Nigerian lesson-note practices.

Its primary purpose is to help teachers move from:

**“What am I teaching next?” → “Prepare the lesson” → “Teach it” → “Record what happened” → “Improve the next lesson.”**

The app combines:

Nigerian lesson-note formats School schemes of work Curriculum-aligned content Teacher-provided materials Textbooks and references  
AI-assisted lesson generation Lesson history and continuity Classroom activities  
Assessment and question creation Teaching aids

Remedial and revision planning

School collaboration and review Teacher-created resource libraries Public teacher resource sharing Professional-development support

The teacher remains the **final decision-maker** throughout the workflow.

**2\. Product Vision**

Create the most useful lesson-preparation companion for Nigerian teachers by making lesson planning:

Faster

More context-aware  
More aligned with actual classroom progress Easier to review and improve

Easier to reuse  
More collaborative where appropriate More connected to teaching resources  
Flexible enough to accommodate different schools and teaching styles The app should not simply generate generic lesson notes.

It should understand:

**the school → teacher → class → subject → scheme → topic → previous lessons → classroom experience → next lesson.**

**4\. Core Product Principle**

**Teacher remains in control**  
AI should **assist rather than replace professional judgment**. Teachers should always be able to:

Edit Reject Replace  
Regenerate Override Reuse Compare Restore Save

Share

Keep private

Lesson Prep should never silently change teacher content because it thinks another version is better.

**6\. Lesson Creation**

Teachers should be able to start a lesson in multiple ways.

**Full preparation**

The teacher provides the relevant information and requests a complete lesson note.

**Quick preparation**

The teacher can provide minimal information such as:

“JSS2 Mathematics — Algebra tomorrow.”

Lesson Prep uses existing context and asks only for essential missing information.

**Textbook-driven preparation**

The teacher can start from:

A textbook chapter A textbook section A textbook page

A photograph of textbook content

**Existing-note-driven preparation**

The teacher can start from:

Previous lesson Existing lesson note Uploaded old note Similar previous lesson

**Scheme-driven preparation**

Lesson Prep can use the teacher's:

Scheme of work School scheme Curriculum  
Term plan Timetable

**7\. Generation Modes**

What needs revision

**29\. Conflicting Sources**

When curriculum, school schemes, textbooks, or external information differ, Lesson Prep should:

1\.	Identify the difference.

2\.	Show the relevant sources.  
3\. Explain why the difference may matter. 4\. Let the teacher choose what to use.

5\.	Never silently replace school-approved content.

**82\. Key Product Differentiators**

Lesson Prep should differentiate itself through the combination of:

**Nigerian-first lesson preparation**

Built around Nigerian teacher workflows rather than generic lesson planning.

**School-aware preparation**

School schemes, templates, resources, and instructions are treated as important sources of truth.

**Class-aware preparation**

Lesson Prep prepares for the actual class, not just the class name.

**Continuity**

Lesson Prep remembers where teaching stopped and what students struggled with.

**Teacher-controlled AI**

AI assists without taking ownership of the teacher's professional decisions.

**Deep reuse**

Teachers can reuse whole lessons or individual components.

**Source transparency**

Teachers can understand where content came from.

**Collaboration without loss of ownership**

Teachers can collaborate while preserving private work and original versions.

**Public teacher knowledge**

Teachers can optionally share their expertise beyond their own school.

**Full teaching cycle**

Preparation connects naturally to teaching, reflection, remediation, revision, and future preparation.

**83\. Explicitly Out of Initial Scope**

One major feature decision was deliberately **not** selected for the current initial feature set:

**Student report/progress insights**

Lesson Prep should **not initially become a student-reporting or student- performance analytics platform**.

The app can use teacher-recorded classroom difficulties and assessment information to improve lesson preparation, but broader student reporting/progress analytics can be reconsidered later.
