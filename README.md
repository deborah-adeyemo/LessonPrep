# LessonPrep

Personal Nigerian lesson-preparation and teaching-support app for teachers.

> “Lesson Prep is your personal lesson-preparation assistant. It knows what you teach, who you teach, what you have already taught, how you like to prepare, and what happened in your previous lessons — and it helps you prepare the next lesson while leaving every decision in your hands.”

## What it is

Lesson Prep helps Nigerian primary and secondary school teachers create, organize, improve, reuse, and prepare lesson notes independently — no HOD approval, no school admin workflow, no supervision required.

Core loop:

**Choose what you are teaching → Create your lesson → Review it → Teach → Record what happened → Improve it → Prepare the next lesson.**
